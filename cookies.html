Cookie Policy

MatchupCoach.gg · Effective date: June 12, 2026 · Last updated: September 23, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how MatchupCoach.gg uses cookies and similar technologies (such as local storage) when you use our website. It should be read together with our Privacy Policy.

1. The Short Version

MatchupCoach.gg sets no cookies. Not one. Not for analytics, not for advertising, not even to sign you in. There is no Google Analytics, no advertising or retargeting pixel, no session-replay recorder, and no third-party tracking script anywhere on this site. That is why you have never been shown a cookie banner here: there is nothing to consent to.

Cookies are small text files a website stores on your device through your browser, and they are the usual way a site follows a person from page to page. We use none. What the site does use is your browser's own local storage, which behaves differently: the values stay on your device, only matchupcoach.gg can read them, they are not attached to every request the way a cookie is, and they are not used to build a profile of anyone. Below is every single one, by name — four in total, and nothing else. We use no IndexedDB and no other browser storage.

2a. The One Thing We Count

We keep a single tally on our own server, written by our own code: for each calendar day, how many times each of a fixed list of twelve events happened. A stored row is an event name, a date, and a whole number — for example, "a matchup guide was opened, on this date, forty times." It records no cookie, no IP address, no user agent, no referrer, no account name, no session token, and no device, browser, or visitor identifier. Nothing in it is tied to you, nothing distinguishes one visitor from another, and no individual can be reconstructed from it, because there are no per-person rows in it at all.

Here is the entire list. Nine are counted when your browser reports them; three are counted by our own server when it does the thing itself, so we can tell how much of the first nine gets eaten by ad blockers:

Two labels may be attached to a count, and only these two: the lane (top, mid, bot, support, or jungle) and the plan (lane, all, or year). Both are fixed lists, so no other text — and no identifier — can ever reach the file through them. There is no visitor id here, no fingerprint, and no "anonymous" hash of anything about you: plenty of sites send one of those and call it privacy-friendly, and we send none.

Counts are kept for 180 days and then dropped. They are readable only by us, over a private endpoint that is switched off unless a secret key is configured, and they are never sold, shared, or handed to anyone else. This is the only usage measurement this site has.

Separately, our server sees your IP address for the instant a request takes and holds it in memory for up to 60 seconds to rate-limit sign-in attempts and block automated abuse. It is never written to disk, never logged, and never attached to your account.

3. Third Parties

A few things are loaded from, or handed off to, other companies, and those run under their own policies rather than ours:

  • Riot Games' Data Dragon and Google Fonts serve champion images and fonts. These are plain file downloads; we pass them nothing about you.
  • Stripe handles checkout and the billing portal. Those pages are Stripe's own, and Stripe sets its own cookies there — not here.
  • Google Sign-In runs only if you choose it, and sets its own cookies on Google's domain.

3a. Your Consent and Choices

Because the site places no cookies and runs no analytics, tracking, or advertising technology, there is no non-essential storage to consent to and nothing to opt out of. The values above are what make signing in and the daily free matchup work; clearing them signs you out and does nothing else.

4. Managing Cookies

  • Clearing what we store. Use your browser's "clear site data" for matchupcoach.gg, or its developer tools, to delete the values above. That signs you out and resets today's free pick. Nothing on your account is lost.
  • Blocking storage. Most browsers let you block site data for one site. If you block it for matchupcoach.gg you can still read the site, but you will not be able to stay signed in.
  • Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control. There is nothing here for these signals to switch off: we do not track, profile, or advertise to anyone, whether or not a signal is sent.
