How to cancel

Three taps. No phone calls, no retention flows, no questions asked.

  1. Sign in at matchupcoach.gg
  2. Click “Manage membership” — it's in the top-right corner, next to your name
  3. Click “Cancel subscription” on the billing page that opens
Sign in and cancel →

What happens when you cancel: nothing is charged again, and you keep full access until the end of the period you already paid for — the end of the month on monthly plans, the end of the year on the annual plan. No partial-period refunds except as described in the Refund Policy (your first-ever payment has a 7-day money-back guarantee).

Can’t sign in, or the button doesn’t work?

Email support@matchupcoach.gg from the address on your account with the word “cancel” — we’ll cancel it for you, same effect, no questions.
