3. Cookies and Tracking

MatchupCoach.gg sets no cookies at all. There are no analytics cookies, no marketing or advertising cookies, no tracking pixels, and no third-party tracking or advertising scripts anywhere on the site — which is why you have never seen a cookie banner here. There is nothing to consent to.

To keep you signed in, and to remember the one matchup a free account unlocks each day, the site stores a few values in your own browser's local storage. Those stay on your device, are readable only by matchupcoach.gg, and are not used to profile you. Every one of them is listed by name, with what it holds, in our Cookie Policy — there are four, and there is nothing else. Stripe's checkout pages and Google's sign-in page run on their own domains and set their own cookies there, under their own policies.

  • Legal and safety — to comply with applicable law, a lawful request, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of MatchupCoach, our users, or the public.
  • Business transfers — if we are involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to this Policy.

5. Data Retention

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes described in this Policy, to provide the Service, to comply with our legal, tax, and accounting obligations, and to resolve disputes. When you delete your account, we delete or anonymize your personal information within a reasonable period, except where we must retain certain records (for example, transaction records) to meet legal requirements.

The daily event counts described in Section 1 are kept for 180 days and then dropped automatically. They are not personal information and there is nothing in them to delete on request, because nothing in them refers to a person. Your IP address is discarded within 60 seconds, as described above.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect personal information, including password hashing (PBKDF2), encrypted connections (HTTPS), and access controls. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
